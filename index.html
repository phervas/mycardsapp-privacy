Privacy Policy for My Cards

Last updated: September 22, 2026

My Cards is designed without ads or advertising tracking. Your card information stays on your device and, if enabled, in your own iCloud account.

1. Where Your Card Information Is Stored

Your card information is stored locally on your Apple device.

If iCloud sync is enabled, your card information may also be stored in your personal iCloud account so it can sync across your Apple devices.

iCloud storage and sync are provided by Apple. My Cards does not have access to your iCloud account or the card information stored there.

2. How Your Card Information Is Used

Your card information is processed locally on your device to provide the app’s core features, such as:

  • Saving your cards
  • Displaying your barcodes or QR codes
  • Organizing and searching your cards
  • Showing your cards when you need to scan them
  • Syncing your cards across your Apple devices through iCloud, if enabled

Because we do not store this information on our servers, we cannot view, recover, or restore your cards for you.

3. Card Information We Do Not Receive

We do not receive or store your loyalty card information on servers we control.

This includes:

  • Card numbers
  • Barcodes
  • QR codes
  • Images or screenshots used to add or import cards

We do not sell your data or share your card information with stores, brands, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Analytics and Tracking

My Cards does not track you across apps or websites.

My Cards does not use analytics for advertising or advertising profiling.

We use usage analytics to understand how My Cards is used, improve its features, and identify technical issues.

We use:

  • Amplitude for product analytics
  • Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting

For Amplitude, data is processed using its EU infrastructure. IP address and location collection are disabled.

For Google Firebase, IDFA collection and Google Signals are disabled. Granular location and device data collection is also disabled, and analytics data is not used for ads personalization. Google Analytics may still collect country and region-level metadata by default.

Analytics do not include your loyalty card numbers, barcodes, QR codes, or images. Analytics may use technical identifiers that are not linked to your identity to measure app usage over time.

Analytics and diagnostic data is retained for no longer than 24 months.

5. Third-Party Services

My Cards uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud, for syncing your cards across your Apple devices
  • Amplitude, for product analytics
  • Google Firebase, for analytics and crash reporting

Your loyalty card numbers, barcodes, QR codes, or images are not sent to Amplitude or Google Firebase.

My Cards does not use advertising services or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can delete your cards at any time inside the app.

You can manually back up and export your cards at any time inside the app.

You can manage iCloud sync from your Apple device settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal data processed through our analytics and diagnostic services, including rights of access, deletion, restriction, or objection.

For privacy questions or requests, you can contact us using the email address below.

If you are in the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
